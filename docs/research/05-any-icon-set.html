Any icon set: six more sets, same patterns?

Can Variance take any icon set, rebuild it, and correct it toward a variable font? Six more sets: Lucide and Tabler (drawn as strokes), Phosphor (five weights), Fluent (six sizes, regular and filled), Heroicons (outline, solid at three sizes) and Remix (line and fill).

Follows research 04. Code: prototype/rebuild/anyset.py, edgemove.py, pairs.py, fillfp.py, gtruth.py, fig05.py in snds/variance.

Yes: the same patterns show up in every set, and the tool works on sets it has never seen.

The fitter passes an answer-key test. Lucide and Tabler ship their true centre-lines. Given only the outline, the fitter got all 300 icons exact and put a median 97 to 98% of ink in rules. Its centre-lines sit about 0.02 px from the real ones.

Every icon now rebuilds exactly. The first run had 291 of 300 line icons and 188 of 200 filled icons. All 21 misses were the geometry engine, not the fitter, and are fixed (see "Closing the last misses").

That test caught a real bug. Whole circles came out as zero-length arcs. Fixed, three research 03 icons rose by 22 to 57 points.

Weight has two house styles. Phosphor's weights are one centre-line drawn with a wider or narrower pen: a single rule explains 99.9% of thin and light. Material splits the change by context instead. The five correction kinds repeat in every set; the amounts are each set's style, learnable from one pair of masters.

FILL is the same recipe in all six sets. Flood the counters, turn inner detail strokes into knockouts one stroke wide, and carry slash cuts through. The main exception is a counter left open on purpose.

Small sizes are hand work everywhere. Heroicons and Carbon redraw their 16 px icons; Fluent partly does.

How it works

  1. One loader for any SVG. Each shape is read with its own fill, stroke and fill rule. Strokes are outlined with their pen. Invisible helper shapes are dropped.
  2. Measure edge moves from pixels. Research 04 followed points, which needs masters with identical points. Other sets don't have them. So for every edge pixel of one style, we measure how far the other style's edge is, along the edge's normal. Same contexts and same five kinds as research 04.
  3. Check it on Material first. On 300 Material icons, the pixel method finds the same rules as research 04's exact edges on the stroke contexts that cover most edges: within 0.05 px at weight (outside edge at wght 700: +0.88 px against +0.86 px), within 0.15 px at size. Solids and stroke ends differ, because pixels can't tell them apart.
  4. Compare filled styles with "flood every counter of the outline".
  5. Run an answer-key test on the two sets that ship their centre-lines.

  • Flood: the filled icon is the outline with its counters filled.
  • Knockout: an inner detail stroke becomes a gap through the fill. In all six sets the knockout is as wide as the outline's stroke (median ratio 0.98 to 1.00): the detail stroke itself is cut out.
  • Cut: a slash's gap carries on through the fill.
  • Open counter: one counter is left empty on purpose (Phosphor's acorn keeps its body open).
  • Bolder: the filled style's strokes are heavier. Mostly Fluent.
  • Reshape: open shapes are closed, so a chevron becomes a triangle. Mostly Phosphor.
  • Generated = flood + knockout + cut. Variance can make all three from the skeleton.

Closing the last misses

The first run left 9 line icons and 12 filled icons short of exact. In all 21 the fit was fine; for some it was already exact before patching. The miss came from the last step. Skia's boolean difference, used to build the fix-up patch, returned a wrong shape where two outlines share nearly the same edge, so the patch added or removed the wrong ink.

The patch step now tries several ways, in order: exact booleans; booleans on copies rounded to 1/16, 1/4 and 1 unit; and last, a patch traced from the pixel difference. It keeps whichever scores best, and never keeps a patch that makes the icon worse.

  • All 500 icons are now exact. 61 needed no patch, 426 closed with exact booleans, and 13 needed a rounded copy (1/16 unit in 11, coarser in 2). None needed the traced fallback.
  • Research 03 is now 40 of 40. Its two remaining misses, fingerprint-recognition and logo--kubernetes, are exact too. logo--kubernetes rose from 45% to 88% rules.

What this means for Variance

  1. Import any set. Outline it (strokes with their own pen), fit the skeleton, keep the fit as the source. It works on stroke-drawn and filled sets alike.
  2. Learn each set's rule from a second style. One pair of masters gives the context table. With none, offer presets: centred (Phosphor), anchored (Material), per-size layout (Fluent).
  3. Add one source flag for FILL: open. A counter that stays empty at FILL 1. The rest of FILL is already in the format: flood counters, detail strokes become knockouts of the same width, cuts carry through.
  4. Start the FILL midpoint test from "counters close around details". Every set's FILL 1 looks like research 01's third option.
  5. Plan hand work for 16 px. It's a redraw in Carbon and Heroicons, and partly in Fluent. Larger sizes and weights are mostly rules, resizes and anchors.
